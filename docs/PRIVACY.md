## On your device

Imported CSV, TSV and text files are copied into the app document library.
Document names, cell contents, row and column counts, and last-opened dates are
stored locally. Editing changes the app copy.

## Files and exports

Import reads files you choose through Files. Export writes a CSV copy to the
destination you select. A cloud file provider may synchronize or retain files
under its own policies. The app does not operate its own spreadsheet server.

## Removing data

Delete a document in the app list to remove the app copy. This does not delete
the original imported file or an exported copy. Deleting the app removes its
local library. External files, provider copies and device backups may remain.

## Developer services

This version contains no developer-run analytics, advertising or account
service. Device backups and services you choose, such as sharing destinations or
file providers, follow their own settings and policies.

## Text CSV for Android

Text CSV for Android stores the documents you create or import in the app's
private library on your device. Document contents are not sent to Plain Text
Office. It has no accounts, advertising or analytics. Import reads only the
files you choose, and export writes a copy to a destination you choose; files
kept with a cloud provider are handled by that provider.

Google Play handles the optional Text CSV Premium subscription under Google's
terms. Plain Text Office does not receive your payment card details, but Google
provides order information such as the order number, product, price and billing
country. The app includes Google's billing library, which has internet access
and may send diagnostic information about purchases to Google. The app keeps a
local record of whether your account has Premium so it works offline;
uninstalling removes that record, while the subscription stays with your Google
account until you cancel it in Google Play.

If you choose Feedback, the app builds a message from your note, the app
version, Android version and device model and opens your email app with a draft
addressed to <support@plaintxtoffice.com>; if no email app is installed, it
hands the message to an app you choose. You can also open our contact page
instead. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and we use feedback to reply, fix
bugs and consider feature requests. Document contents are never included.

Cloud backup of app data is turned off; on some phones, a device-to-device
transfer during setup may still copy it. Uninstalling the app removes its
library; imported originals and exported copies remain where you saved them. If
you share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google may give us aggregated crash
and performance information.

## Apple diagnostics

We intend to use Apple-provided crash and performance information, when
available under your Apple diagnostic-sharing choices, only to troubleshoot
problems and improve reliability. App diagnostic logging should not
intentionally include document text, cell contents, document names, private
filenames, URLs, tokens or other private content. Apple controls its
system-generated reports; we cannot guarantee those reports contain no sensitive
information.

## Questions

Contact opens our website. We use the name, email address and message you
provide to respond to your request. Service providers may process this
correspondence for us. We retain it as needed to resolve the request or meet
legal obligations. Use the same contact form to request deletion.

Contact Plain Text Office: <https://plaintxtoffice.com/contact/>
